Data Deletion & Privacy Policy

At MediSave, we support your right to control your personal data. You can delete your account directly from within the app (go to **Profile** → **Settings** → **Account** → **Delete Account**). If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your account, you can request permanent deletion using the form below.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once processed, all of your cloud backups, local health reminders, dose logs, and family connections will be permanently destroyed.

What data will be deleted?

  • Your profile and email details from Firebase Authentication.
  • Your entire cloud document and profile in the Firestore Database.
  • All of your medication reminders and custom slots.
  • Your complete medication adherence logs and historical charts.
  • Active caregiver connections and pending invitations (severing all synchronization).
  • All local SQLite database files and caches on your physical device.

What data is retained?

None. We do not retain any health data, medication histories, or logs after account deletion has been completed.
